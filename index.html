The Story Behind the Code

How curiosity about video games turned into a passion for building resilient software systems and compelling worlds.

The Journey

My journey into technology began in 7th grade, the day my dad brought home our family's first computer.

Like any kid, I loved video games and exploring the internet. But what fascinated me most wasn't just playing, it was the mystery behind the screen: How does a massive 10 GB open world run smoothly on just 4 GB of RAM? How do millions of lines of code talk to physical computer chips in milliseconds?

That curiosity pushed me to study computer science. My first programming language was C++. It was tough, but the moment the logic clicked, I was hooked. I went on to earn my Master of Computer Applications in AI & Machine Learning with an 8.7 CGPA. Today, I design backend systems, clean up legacy codebases, and train smart AI models, still driven by that same excitement to see how things work under the hood.

To me, writing code is like building an engine: every part has to work cleanly together, handle sudden traffic without stalling, and deliver a smooth, effortless ride for the person using it.
